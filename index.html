About

I build AI systems that work outside the notebook — models that understand Indic languages, assistants that respond in real time, and the infrastructure that keeps them running.

I hold a Bachelor's in Electronics & Telecommunication with honours in AI/ML, which gave me a foundation that spans signals and hardware as much as deep learning. Today I'm a researcher at the Centre for Artificial Intelligence & Robotics (CAIR), IIT Mandi.

Before that, at YellowSense Technologies, I developed generative models with speech-to-text and text-to-speech for Hindi, Bengali and Marathi, alongside the APIs and databases behind them. I'm most drawn to LLMs, applied research and well-engineered backends.
