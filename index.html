Ongoing researchCAIR, IIT Mandi2024 —

Camera-only mapping for mobile robots

Autonomous mobile robots usually lean on LiDAR or depth sensors to understand a room. This work asks how far one 360° camera can go on its own. Monocular imagery is reconstructed into a 3D Gaussian Splatting map, and that map is used to detect obstacles for navigation.

  • 360° vision
  • 3D Gaussian Splatting
  • Monocular mapping
  • Obstacle detection
  • AMR

  1. aCapture

    One equirectangular 360° frame. No LiDAR, no depth.

  2. bReconstruct

    Build a 3D Gaussian Splatting map from monocular imagery.

  3. cDetect

    Find obstacles in the reconstructed map.

  4. dNavigate

    Plan a path around what the camera found.
