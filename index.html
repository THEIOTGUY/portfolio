01About

From electronics to robots that see.

I started in electronics, moved through language models and speech, and now build the eyes of mobile robots.

Hello! I'm Ayush. My degree is in Electronics & Telecommunication with honours in AI/ML, which gave me a foundation in deep learning, NLP and computer vision, and in the signals and hardware that robotics runs on.

Today I'm an AI & Robotics researcher at CAIR, IIT Mandi. Before that, at YellowSense Technologies, I built generative models with speech-to-text and text-to-speech for Indic languages, along with the APIs and databases behind them. I'm driven by LLMs, AI research and well-engineered backends.

03Projects

Selected work.

Ongoing CAIR, IIT Mandi

Camera-only mapping for mobile robots

Perception for autonomous mobile robots using just one 360° camera, with no LiDAR or depth sensors. Monocular imagery is reconstructed into a 3D Gaussian Splatting map, which is then used to detect obstacles for navigation.

  • Monocular mapping
  • Obstacle detection
